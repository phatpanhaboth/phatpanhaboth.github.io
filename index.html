I am an economics researcher based in Phnom Penh, working as an Economics and Financial Officer at Cambodia's Non-Bank Financial Services Authority. I graduated with high distinction in International Economics from the Royal University of Phnom Penh. My research looks at how institutional quality shapes foreign direct investment across sectors in Southeast Asia. Alongside my work, I am an Editorial Assistant at the Cambodian Education Forum and Curator of the Global Shapers Community, Phnom Penh Hub, an initiative of the World Economic Forum.
